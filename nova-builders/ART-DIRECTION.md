# Nova architectural art direction

The redesign uses warm ivory (#f5f2eb), charcoal (#222824), stone (#e8e2d7) and muted bronze (#796449). At the user's request, the hero keeps the original twilight Aurelian image, ivory (#f3f0eb) headline and lilac (#c9b9d7) italic emphasis in the original Cormorant typography. Photography carries the visual identity; UI accents stay restrained. Project-first sequence, landscape imagery, useful specifications, and plainly labelled fictional content replace generic luxury claims.

## New image provenance

Produced with the built-in image-generation tool, following the imagegen skill. Originals are preserved beside responsive WebP derivatives in dist/assets. Existing tower concepts and both video files are retained.

## Final prompt set

### daylight

Use case: lighting-weather. Asset: daylight project photograph for fictional Nova Builders architectural portfolio. Edit the supplied Aurelian tower image: change only time of day and lighting to clear soft late-morning coastal daylight. Preserve the exact tower design, planted terraces, foreground landscape, camera position, composition and aspect ratio. Warm pale stone, charcoal metal, natural glass, restrained greenery, pale blue sky. Editorial architectural photography, rich realistic stone detail, crisp but not oversharpened, no purple cast, no text or logos.

### interior

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: wide luxury residential interior photograph for Nova Builders fictional portfolio. A generously proportioned contemporary apartment lounge with floor-to-ceiling glazing and a planted terrace, distant coastal city visible outside. Honed pale limestone, warm oak wall panels, off-white linen sofa, one dark bronze coffee table, quiet sculptural furnishing. Soft natural daylight, subtle real surface texture, architectural editorial photography, landscape 3:2 composition, no people, no text, no branding, no dramatic purple or gold lighting.

### materials

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: wide architectural material-detail photograph for Nova Builders fictional portfolio. Close-up of a beautifully resolved contemporary building entrance: warm beige travertine wall meets vertically ribbed dark bronze door frame and oak ceiling, small area of ornamental grass at edge. Oblique perspective, sunlight across textured stone, fine honest construction joints, restrained warm neutral palette, tactile editorial architecture photography, landscape 3:2. No text, no logos, no people, no purple cast.

## Boundaries

These are illustrative fictional concepts, not verified real developments or a coherent construction documentation set. The 3D model and blueprint communicate design process and are independently labelled studies. The consultation form creates a local draft only. No video re-encoding, Git push or deployment is part of this redesign.
